Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Kids Shorts Uploader (“the App”) is a local desktop helper for uploading and managing videos on the developer’s own YouTube channel.

Data we access

When you authorize the App with Google, it may access YouTube account data needed to:

  • Upload videos you choose
  • Set titles, descriptions, tags, and audience settings
  • Update channel description/branding settings you request

How data is used

YouTube data is used only to perform actions you start on your own computer. The App does not sell personal data. OAuth tokens are stored locally in your project credentials/ folder on your device.

Data storage & sharing

We do not operate a separate cloud backend for this tool. Video files stay on your machine until you upload them to YouTube. We do not share your Google data with third parties except Google/YouTube as required to provide the API features you use.
